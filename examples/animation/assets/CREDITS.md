The lamp is modelled, rigged and animated from scratch by `../props.py`, the chest by `../chest.py`.
The chest's wood and iron are two [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com) scans, [rough_wood](https://polyhaven.com/a/rough_wood)
and [rusty_metal_04](https://polyhaven.com/a/rusty_metal_04) (CC0), which `chest.py` downloads at run time rather than
shipping them here.
