# Kiln Studio's film — the pictures

The pictures the film in the site's Kiln Studio section is cut from. Its source (the HyperFrames
compositions, the storyboard, the capture scripts) is in the main branch, in
`site/assets-src/studio-film/`: copy `assets/` next to it to render it again.

- `studio/`: Kiln Studio's own page, captured at 3840 × 2160 during the real session — the mascot,
  the note pinned on its hammer, the note addressed, the compare.
- `session/`: the views Claude looked at in its own Blender during that session.
- `ui/`: Studio's pin, note and button, cut out of the same captures.
- `intro/`: Studio on a demo project with four kiln 3.0 models (the ammo box open). In `i5-qr.png`
  the QR code is pixelated and the address under it blurred.
- `render/`: rendered in Blender from the real files — the hammer before and after, Studio's
  compare sweep, the mascot's walk with its feet flat.
- `brand/`: the blender-kiln logo. `fonts/`: Sora and JetBrains Mono, both under the SIL Open
  Font License 1.1.
