# Design System: blender-kiln

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Forge, Cinematic"**

The page is night, and the only light in it comes from the fire. A kiln-made forge (the logo, built in 3D by kiln itself) burns in a full-bleed WebGL scene; the scroll is the camera, falling into the forge, watching objects rise out of it and get graded against the real thing. The document is not a page with a 3D frame in it: the scene is the page, and the type stands beside the fire, never over it.

Everything else is lit by that fire or sits in shadow. Ember and molten are light sources, not decoration: they glow (bloom on emissive meshes, ember halos on the primary action and the "after" bars, a text glow on the ratio), and the ground stays a warm near-black. Film grain lies fixed over the whole viewport. Clash Display is set huge and tight; Geist carries the reading; Geist Mono carries every measured number. Controls are pills, and evidence sits on double-bezel plates like objects on a stand.

Motion is scroll-scrubbed and pinned (GSAP ScrollTrigger on Lenis smooth scroll), and every pinned scene has a reduced-motion form that is a plain, readable layout rather than a frozen frame.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Night ground (#0b0908) lit only by ember, molten and kiln's flame; slate is the forge's stone.
- One continuous WebGL forge driving the first act; the camera view is offset so the headline never covers the fire.
- Clash Display at cinematic sizes, Geist for text, Geist Mono for every number.
- Pill controls and a floating pill nav; double-bezel plates (6px shell around a core).
- Translucent bone and ember alphas as named tints, never opaque greys.
- The page ends at the real fire: the fixed WebGL forge banks low behind the reading sections and flares again, right of the type, for the closing install. No CSS gradient stands in for forge light.

## Colors

A warm night lit from within: three fire hues as light, warm bone and ash for text, and alpha tints of bone and ember in place of a grey ramp.

### Primary
- **Molten** (molten): the hottest readable light. Primary action fill, active chip, nav call to action, progress rail, the "hot" word in a headline, specimen names, the current lantern tick, focus ring, link hover.
- **Ember** (ember): the fire itself. Text selection, the "after" bar gradient start, the ÷ glyph in the ratio, and (as alpha) every glow, halo and floor wash on the page.

### Secondary
- **Flame** (flame) and **Flame Lit** (flame-lit): kiln's own orange from the logo. Brand mark color; flame-lit is the link hover inside the bench's long-form prose.
- **Forge Slate** (slate): the forge's stone, carried by the kiln-made GLB and its renders rather than by CSS.

### Tertiary (bench overlay only)
- **Truth Red** (truth), **Kiln Cyan** (kiln), **Both** (both): the bench's comparison marking (real object in red, kiln's rebuild in cyan, overlap pale). They exist only inside the bench's overlay rasters and legends.

### Neutral
- **Night** (night): page ground, html background, theme-color, WebGL clear color and fog.
- **Char** (char): command blocks, the install core, glass fallback when transparency is reduced.
- **Soot** (soot): the "before" bar fill and the scrollbar thumb.
- **Bone** (bone): primary text and headlines.
- **Ash** (ash, 11.4:1 on night): secondary text, ledes, nav links at rest.
- **Ash Dim** (ash-dim, 5.8:1 on night): captions, footnotes, figcaptions, tick labels.
- **Ink** (ink): text on molten and ember surfaces (primary buttons, the GitHub pill).
- **Hearth Core** (hearth-core-lit to hearth-core-dark) and **Stage** (stage-lit to stage-dark): the radial dark wells behind specimens on a plate and behind the live creature viewport.
- **Sweep** (sweep): the bench renders' own grey studio ground, allowed only inside a plate core, never as page background.

### Tints (named alpha roles)
- **Bone hairlines** (line, line-strong): every border and inset ring; line-strong for the tick track and the "before" bar ring.
- **Bone washes** (bone-04 to bone-12): plate shells (04), ghost and chip fills (06, hover 10 to 12), nav hover (07), icon wells (08), inset top highlights on cores (10 to 12).
- **Ember glows** (ember-glow-10, -12, -22): radial floor light under a section (Lanterns, Animate, the bench header).
- **Ember halos** (ember-halo-45, -60, -70): outer glow under lit things (after bars, primary action).
- **Molten rings** (molten-ring-40, molten-trail-45): the primary action's 1px ring; past lantern ticks.
- **Ink tints** (ink-10 to ink-25): controls and the install shell on the fire, where bone washes would vanish.
- **Glass and night veils** (glass, night-60, -75, -92): nav glass, the "now playing" pill, the theatre vignette, the narrow-screen reading scrim.

### Named Rules
**The Only Light Rule.** Light comes from the fire. Bright surfaces are molten, ember or the WebGL fire itself; there is no neutral light-grey panel anywhere on the page.

**The Plate Rule.** A render with a grey studio ground is an object on a stand: it sits in a plate core, never on the page ground.

**The Two Marks Rule.** Red and cyan are the bench's marking. They appear only in the bench overlay, never as accent, state or error color on the site.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Clash Display 500/600/700 (fallback Geist Variable, sans-serif)
**Body Font:** Geist Variable (fallback ui-sans-serif, sans-serif)
**Label/Mono Font:** Geist Mono Variable (fallback ui-monospace, monospace), tabular numbers, -0.01em

**Character:** a hard, wide display face set enormous and tight against a neutral, quiet sans; numbers are always mono so a score reads as a measurement, not a slogan.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (600, display-hero / display-close / display-bench): the hero headline (two lines, each rising out of its own mask), the closing word set across the fire, the bench title.
- **Headline** (600, headline and its Method / Lanterns siblings, lh 0.95, -0.025em): section heads and the theatre's chapter lines. Word spacing is opened (0.05 to 0.08em) to hold the tight tracking apart.
- **Title** (600, title-version, title-prose, title-step, title, subtitle, name, name-sm): Method step titles, route titles, the lantern version number and change line, specimen and brief names.
- **Body** (400, body 1.0625rem / 1.6 is the page default; lede fluid; body-lg 1.125rem for the Method intro; body-md 1rem; small 0.875rem; caption 0.8125rem): reading measures stay between 34ch and 56ch (74ch in bench prose).
- **Control** (600, 0.9375rem; 1rem on hero actions): nav links, chips, copy button, close links.
- **Measure** (Geist Mono 500 for scores, 400 elsewhere): the score, lantern score, ratio, install commands, facts, tallies, bar values (0.75rem to 0.875rem).

### Named Rules
**The Measured Number Rule.** Every number on the page is set in Geist Mono with tabular figures and comes from `src/content/site.json`; nothing is typed into a component.

**The Clean Copy Rule.** No em-dashes in visible copy.

## Layout

Full-bleed sections with a fluid gutter (clamp(16px, 4vw, 64px)) and no centered max-width container on the home page; the bench page alone uses a two-column document grid (15rem sticky table of contents, 74ch prose). The 8px unit drives the bench page's rhythm; home sections breathe at 120 to 180px vertical padding.

The home page is a sequence of scroll scenes:
- **Theatre** (560svh, a 100svh sticky stage): the forge scene scrubbed by scroll progress. The hero type sits left, the forge right; on wide screens the camera's view offset shifts the forge right by 22% of the width, on portrait it lowers it, so type and fire never overlap. Chapter lines, then specimen plates with their score, then a tally.
- **Method** (600svh, pinned): the ammo box session replayed on its real GLB in its own WebGL canvas, one step per screen of scroll: thirteen parts assemble from an exploded view with the session's crops and photo floating; the two kept scans orbit and wrap the clay; the camera takes the photo's angle (azimuth 50, elevation 20) and the model in cyan meets the photo's silhouette in red; the camera dives on three joints; the file counts down 5.54 MB to 187 KB. Each step quotes its production-log line verbatim (… marks a cut), with a five-step rail. Smoothing is time-based (1 - e^(-7 dt)) so a slow frame rate never desyncs scene and scroll.
- **Lanterns** (520svh, 480svh under 960px): five turning points (v1, v4, v6, v12, v15) as clean cut-outs between the photo and the real object, over an inline-SVG curve of all fifteen truth scores laid out at its real pixel size: a 2px molten line drawn as the scroll advances, a cursor, 8px+ markers with a night ring, milestones ringed, v12 hollow and dashed and left out of the line, a hover title per point and a screen-reader table.
- **Animate**: a live WebGL viewport beside the routes, then what was left out.
- **Close**: the ratio rows, then the fire.

Breakpoints are 560px, 760px and 960px.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is light, not lift. Surfaces separate by bone hairlines, inset rings and inner top highlights; real depth comes from the WebGL scene (fog, a fire point light, rim light, bloom on emissive meshes only) and from ember halos under lit things. Shadows are long, soft and negative-spread; there are no offset or hard shadows.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Inset ring** (`box-shadow: inset 0 0 0 1px var(--line)`): plates, chips, viewport, command blocks.
- **Core highlight** (`box-shadow: inset 0 1px 1px rgb(255 238 220 / 0.12)`): the inner core of a double bezel.
- **Nav float** (`box-shadow: inset 0 1px 0 rgb(255 238 220 / 0.06), 0 18px 40px -20px rgb(0 0 0 / 0.8)`).
- **Ember lift** (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 1px rgb(255 181 71 / 0.4), 0 20px 50px -18px rgb(255 90 20 / 0.7)`): primary action only.
- **Cut-out shadow** (`drop-shadow(0 24px 24px rgb(0 0 0 / 0.65))`) under the lantern cut-outs, which fade out at their base.
- **Bar glow** (`box-shadow: 0 0 18px rgb(255 90 20 / 0.6)`). The ratio numerals carry no halo: the real fire behind them lights the section.
- **Install drop** (`box-shadow: inset 0 1px 1px rgb(255 238 220 / 0.1), 0 30px 60px -30px rgb(0 0 0 / 0.7)`).

### Named Rules
**The Glow Is Heat Rule.** A glow is ember-colored and sits under something the fire lit. A grey drop shadow under a card is not part of this world.

**The Grain Rule.** Film grain (SVG fractal noise, opacity 0.07, overlay blend) is fixed over the whole viewport and never scrolls or repaints.

## Shapes

Two families. Controls are full pills (999px): nav, buttons, chips, the "now playing" tag, icon wells, bars. Containers are double-bezel plates: a shell with a 6px padding and a translucent bone fill, holding a core whose radius is the shell's minus 6px (plate 1.5rem around an 18px core; stage 2rem; install shell 1.75rem). Smaller insets (command block, bench prose images) take 1rem; the progress rail and bench code blocks are 2px hairlines. Plates in the theatre tilt -2deg, as if set down.

## Components

### Buttons
Molten, round and warm, with an icon well that leans toward the click.
- **Shape:** full pill (999px), asymmetric padding so the icon well sits flush right (8px 8px 8px 22px on hero actions; 6px 6px 6px 16px on the copy button).
- **Primary:** molten fill, ink text, ember lift shadow; a 36px circular icon well in ink-12.
- **Hover / Focus:** fill turns bone; icon well nudges up-right (translate 2px, -1px, scale 1.06) on the spring ease; press scales to 0.98. Focus is a 2px molten outline offset 3px.
- **Ghost:** bone-06 fill, bone text, line-strong inset ring; hover to bone-12.
- **On the fire:** close links use ink-10 fill and an ink-25 ring, hover ink-20.

### Chips
- **Style:** bone-06 fill, ash text, line inset ring, 9px 16px, Geist 500 at 0.9375rem.
- **State:** hover bone-10 with bone text; pressed (aria-pressed) molten with ink text and no ring.

### Cards / Containers
- **Corner Style:** double bezel (plate 1.5rem / core 18px; stage 2rem / core 2rem minus 6px; shell 1.75rem).
- **Background:** shell bone-04 (ink-18 on the fire); core is a radial hearth well, the stage well, the sweep grey for bench renders, or char.
- **Shadow Strategy:** inset ring on the shell, core highlight inside; see Elevation.
- **Internal Padding:** 6px bezel; command block 16px 18px.

### Inputs / Fields
None: the site has no form fields. The install block is a read-only command list with a copy button.

### Navigation
A floating glass pill 16px from the top, centered: glass fill with 16px blur and 140% saturation, line ring, nav float shadow. Clash wordmark at 1rem, ash links at 0.9375rem that light to bone on a bone-07 pill when hovered or current, and a molten GitHub pill. Under 560px the wordmark collapses to the mark and links tighten to 0.875rem. With reduced transparency the glass becomes solid char.

### The Forge Theatre (signature)
A three.js scene on a sticky canvas: the kiln-made `forge.glb`, about 420 additive embers rising from its mouth, an Unreal bloom pass (strength 0.55) on emissive things, ACES tone mapping, and a scroll-driven camera with a view offset that keeps the forge clear of the type. Specimen GLBs rise in their scroll windows and are graded on plates beside a mono score. A soft night vignette guarantees the type always has dark behind it. Reduced motion: no embers, no flicker, the camera snaps to progress; a still image (`forge-still.webp`) stands in when WebGL is unavailable.

### Live Creatures (signature)
Animated kiln GLBs played live in a WebGL viewport on the stage well; each instance is a SkeletonUtils clone so its skin stays bound to its own skeleton. Drag to orbit, idle auto-spin after 1.5s. Reduced motion freezes the mixer and the spin.

### Measured Bars
Before bars in soot with a line-strong ring; after bars in an ember-to-molten gradient with a bar glow; widths scale from site.json and grow on scroll (scrubbed), with the ratio counting up in mono.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** keep the page ground night (#0b0908) and let only molten, ember and the WebGL fire be bright.
- **Do** set controls as pills (999px) and evidence as double-bezel plates (6px shell, core radius = shell minus 6px).
- **Do** use the named bone and ember alpha tints for borders, washes and glows instead of opaque greys.
- **Do** set every number in Geist Mono with tabular figures, sourced from `src/content/site.json`.
- **Do** keep the forge visible beside the type: offset the camera view, never stack type over the fire.
- **Do** give every pinned or scrubbed scene a reduced-motion layout (Method and the theatre jump to the step without easing; embers and flicker stop; Lenis is not started).
- **Do** have kiln make every 3D asset on the page (the forge, specimens, creatures).

### Don't:
- **Don't** put a grey studio render on the page ground; it goes in a plate core.
- **Don't** use red or cyan anywhere but the bench overlay marking.
- **Don't** use em-dashes in visible copy.
- **Don't** type a figure into a component, or show testimonials, user logos or "trusted by" claims.
- **Don't** use grey drop shadows or hard offset shadows; depth is ember glow, inset rings and the scene's own light.
- **Don't** pin a scene without a reduced-motion fallback.
