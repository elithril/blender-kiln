## Read by eye, from the renders

- **scripted-lantern** — matches the brief: 4-pane grille per face, amber glass, candle, ring. Clean.
- **ai-mushrooms** — raw generation only (355,324 tris, 9.9 MB, untextured). Three mushrooms in a row, not a joined cluster; a seam on one cap. The session stopped where rule 6 says it must: asking to decimate 342K → ~4.5K.
- **polyhaven-chair** — correct asset, grounded, 724 tris. Shipped unoptimized: 16.2 MB of 2K textures.
- **pbr-crate** — reads well, but one corner bracket carries a **flat white triangle** on its chamfer, identical under three camera angles while the three matching corners have none: almost certainly a UV defect. Not caught by the session nor by any metric.
- **rigged-character** — T-pose, 31 deform bones, all driving; 0 unweighted vertices; max 4 influences; stretch p99 1.28. Arms short (session: wingspan 88% of height).
- **batch-props** — stool, bucket, rope: all clean, grounded, Draco-compressed.

## What the sessions themselves reported (verified against logs and files)

**Skill behaviour**
1. **Auto mode stops at OPTIMIZE** on 3 of 6 briefs (chair, crate, villager) and asks — so `_final.glb` is written *before* optimization. Headless cannot answer; in interactive use the file is still misnamed until the user replies.
2. **Rule 2 is not followed**: 2–4 screenshots per session against 6–17 `execute_blender_code` calls.
3. **The default Cube** is deleted in some runs, hidden in others — rule 6 applied inconsistently.
4. **Rig validator gap** (found by the session): control bones that are not exported were parents of deform bones; auto weights skipped 48 accessory vertices (belt, pouch).
5. **Batch report durations are estimates**, not timings, and one asset skipped the `running` state in the manifest.

**Docs out of date**
6. SKILL.md sends Pollinations commands to `references/ai-generation.md`, which does not contain them.
7. `gradio_client` 2.7.1 returns `/shape_generation` output as a dict, not a path — the documented copy step breaks.

**External paths broken**
8. **Pollinations answers HTTP 402 after one free image.** The free concept-art path is effectively gone. The session then painted over the service's watermark — the skill should forbid that.
9. **The `tencent/Hunyuan3D-2` Space's `/generation_all` fails server-side** (`NameError`): no free *textured* AI generation. Shape-only works.
10. Hunyuan3D licence excludes the EU — flagged by the session itself, correctly.

**Bench**
